About Mealuto

Mealuto is an iOS app that automatically detects meal photos in your camera roll on-device, so "when and what you ate" stays organized as a visual journal.

"What did I have for lunch last Wednesday?" — easy to look back. "I keep eating the same thing, let me pick something different tonight" — easy to plan ahead. The record builds up from the photos you're already taking.

All image recognition runs locally on your device. Your photos and recognition results are never sent to any external server.

Key Features

Features marked with Plus are unlocked with the Plus plan (a one-time purchase of ¥800). Everything else is free.

Automatic Meal Photo Detection

When you launch the app or start a scan manually, it scans your camera roll, picks out meal photos automatically, and queues them as candidate entries.

Auto Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snack

Uses each photo's capture time to assign meals to breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack automatically. You can freely adjust the time window for each category in Settings — no manual sorting required.

Calendar with Thumbnails

The monthly calendar shows a thumbnail of what you ate each day. "What did I eat last week?" becomes a glance at the calendar.

Fully On-Device

Image recognition runs entirely on-device. Photos and recognition results never leave your device.

Favorites

Mark meals you revisit with a heart. Filter the timeline or map to "favorites only" to look them back up in an instant.

Auto Mode

Skip the review step — detected meal photos go straight into your log. Zero-friction meal journaling.

Map View

Using location EXIF from your photos, revisit "when, where, and what" you ate over the most recent 7 days. Plus unlocks your full map history.

Calorie Tracking Plus

Enter calories per meal — daily summaries help you review your eating habits at a glance.

Extended Scan Range Plus

Expand the free plan's "past 1 week / up to 50 photos" to an unlimited period and up to 200 photos per scan.

Plans

Mealuto offers the core "automatic meal photo detection from your camera roll" as a free feature, and bundles the features that help you dig deeper — look back, plan ahead — into the Plus plan.

Free Plan

Automatic meal photo detection (past 1 week, up to 50 photos), map and automatic address lookup for the most recent 7 days, automatic breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack categorization based on photo time, calendar view with thumbnails, favorites and tag filtering, Auto Mode, all on-device. No ads.

Plus Plan ¥800 one-time

Unlocks: Full map history, calorie tracking with daily summaries, and extended scan range.
